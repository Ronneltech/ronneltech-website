Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Ronneltech Ltd ("Ronneltech", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through our website may be collected, used and handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Ronneltech or submit a consultation request through our website, you may provide information such as your name, company or business name, email address, phone number, service requirements and the contents of your message.

Our website and service providers may also process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and deliver the website, such as IP address, browser or device information, request information and security-related data.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information submitted through the website to respond to enquiries, evaluate consultation requests, communicate about requested services, provide customer support, protect the website from misuse and improve our services and business operations.

3. Consultation Form

Ronneltech uses a third-party form processing service to receive consultation requests submitted through the website. Information entered into the consultation form may therefore be processed by that provider for the purpose of transmitting the enquiry to Ronneltech.

4. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party technology providers to operate, host, secure or support our website and business communications. These providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services.

5. Information Sharing

Ronneltech does not sell personal information submitted through the website. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, process enquiries, protect our systems, comply with applicable legal obligations or establish and protect legal rights.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes and meeting applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

7. Data Security

Ronneltech takes reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include requesting access, correction or deletion of information or objecting to certain processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

Our website may use or link to third-party services. Ronneltech is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties, and their own privacy policies may apply when you use their services.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, business practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
